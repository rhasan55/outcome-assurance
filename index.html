PRECISE OWNERSHIP

Complement the rails. Own the outcome.

Outcome Assurance consumes protocol facts and enterprise evidence. It does not replace authorization, identity, mandate signing, checkout transport, payment credentials, or order lifecycle standards.

LayerWhat it contributesWhat Outcome Assurance adds
AMEX ACEAgent registration, account enablement, Intent Intelligence, payment credentials, Cart Context, purchase protection direction.Membership-aware outcome contract, value routing, independent post-action proof.
GOOGLE AP2Signed checkout/payment mandates and receipts; deterministic verification of authorized constraints.Typed task definition before mandate creation and outcome reconciliation after payment.
UCPCapability negotiation and standardized discovery, checkout, orders, fulfillment and post-purchase operations.Protocol-neutral success criteria and evidence states portable across UCP merchants.
ACPProgrammatic agent–seller checkout, credential relay, order events and merchant-owned processing.Approved meaning, counterfactual value delta, independent verifier and recovery package.

EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE

  • Mandate signatures or cryptographic rails
  • Generic agent observability
  • Escrow, autonomous refunds, or disputes
  • Replacing merchant order systems
